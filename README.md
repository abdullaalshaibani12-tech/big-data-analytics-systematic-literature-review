# big-data-analytics-systematic-literature-review
Supporting research evidence for an MSc Computing dissertation on Big Data Analytics and Business Decision-Making, including study extraction, thematic coding, screening records and quality assessment.

## Supporting Research Evidence

This repository contains supporting evidence for the MSc Computing dissertation titled _Big Data Analytics and Business Decision-Making: A Systematic Literature Review of Organisational Value, Opportunities and Challenges._

The dissertation synthesises 18 selected studies across five analytical themes:

1. Contribution of BDA to Business Decision-Making
2. Applications of BDA Across Business Functions
3. Technological Enablers of BDA
4. Challenges and Barriers to BDA Implementation
5. Organisational Context and Differences in BDA Outcomes

The accompanying Excel workbook contains six worksheets covering study extraction, initial coding, code-to-theme mapping, study-by-theme associations, screening summaries and methodological quality assessments.

**Evidence provenance:** The extraction and quality-assessment worksheets summarise information reported in the dissertation. The initial codes and thematic associations were reconstructed from the dissertation's written synthesis and should not be interpreted as the original chronological coding log. The screening records present aggregate PRISMA figures rather than individual screening decisions.

The repository is intended to improve transparency and traceability of the thematic synthesis.
